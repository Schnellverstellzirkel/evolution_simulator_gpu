# Round 5, GA/QD: convergence

## 1. The design I back

Breeding on the CPU in cpu's fixed-array form with the counter RNG and the 12-uniform gaussian (a changed stream is a new seed); genes DMA'd per block into VRAM and unpacked by a prologue kernel (ruling 2); persistent per-class kernels with data's bucketed queues and the three retirement rules (5), VRAM tail and drain flag (3), registered host memory and whole-block re-runs after a fault (6); block size and ring depth as experiment constants saved with the experiment (4); reserved slots for the fine kernel and the replay, in-kernel confirmation out (1); the physics spec with the red team's fixes and the L0 to L2.5 ladder with L2.5 as the primary row (7, 8); the rung ladder R1 to R4 with the audit lane, nurseries and immigrants exempt from R1 and R2 at first, R4 floored at the emitter's own median d(10) (10); the growth-step rule as the cap candidate, owner's decision after the dump, W = 8 last (9); the host tax carried at 15 to 21% until os's rows exist (11); the smaller items (12) and the disk rules (13). I reject no ruling. On 4 I add one line: emitter tells per 200 absorbed samples are independent of block size and ring depth, so the search's determinism rests only on the saved constants and ring order, as the ruling requires.

## 2. Tracks, final form

Each track is one coding agent, one to two weeks at most, one worktree, on by default when merged, deleted whole if its gate fails.

T-ga-0, the dump and its reader (shared with ml). Worktree `claude/dump`. Files: `src/worker.rs` (the `EVOLUTION_DUMP_GENERATION=<path>` env var: one all-audit generation, rungs off, bar minus infinity, then the rows and unset), `src/storage.rs` (the per-creature and per-elite rows, section 6 of my round 3), `examples/dump_stats.rs` (mine: the three tail numbers of ruling 9, nursery `entered` counts, cell match between 5, 10 and 20 s, entrant recall per candidate factor, mean final / d(10) of entrants). ml owns the four rung distances in `Result` and `examples/ladder_replay.rs` in the same worktree. Deliverable: one 3M dump from a generation-50 save on the current kernel and the two readers. Gate: the dump loads, the readers print, and `dump_stats` reproduces the ring histogram I measured (8.7 mean, p90 12). Dependencies: ml's `Result` change. Order: first; everything else in this list reads it.

T-ga-1, the streaming arena bar. Worktree `claude/bar-stream`. Files: `src/storage.rs` only (`next_screen`, `arm_screen_early`, `absorb`: a sliding window of the last `RING_SLOTS` screen distances, the bar recomputed at every absorption and fixed into the block bred there; no per-generation bar). Deliverable: the bar moves per block. Gate: kept share per block in `EVOLUTION_STAGE_LOG` within 18 to 22% from generation 2 on and within one generation after a world change; a fixed seed gives one history twice; `search_ab --gpu` at equal time over 10 seeds, best and QD not below control. Dependencies: none. Order: second, one to two days, mergeable alone.

T-ga-2, the bar table. Worktree `claude/bar-table`. Files: `src/physics.rs` (`Screen` becomes a table: entry 0 minus infinity, then per-arena bars, then per-cell bars per rung), `src/creature_kernel.rs` (a u16 bar index and a flags byte per lane record, a table pointer in `Params`), `shaders/warp_creature.cu` and later the per-lane kernel (compare against `bars[index]` at the rung step; the audit and exemption flags), `src/storage.rs` (build the table per block from the arena windows), `src/qd.rs` (`Elite` stores its distance at 150, 300 and 600 steps). Deliverable: per-arena bars in the game; the nursery competes against itself. Gate: with a one-entry table, bit-identical histories to T-ga-1; nursery `Graduation.kept` above zero per cohort on a mature save; rate on `p2_speed` within 1% of before. Dependencies: T-ga-1; ml's `Result` rung floats (T-ga-0). Order: third.

T-ga-3, per-cell bars at R3 (and at R2 once ml's R2 lands). Worktree `claude/cell-bars`. Files: `src/storage.rs` (the neighbourhood minimum over the parent's cell and its 81 neighbours of the elites' rung distances, times ml's calibrated factor; the arena bar when the parent cell is unknown or the slot is a nursery or an immigrant). Deliverable: cell-local screening. Gate, in order: `dump_stats` shows entrants below 0.7 times their neighbourhood minimum at 5 s under 4% of entrants and none of the top 1%; then `search_ab --gpu` at equal time over 10 seeds, best at or above control, QD at or above 0.95x, steps per creature at or below 0.8x. Dependencies: T-ga-2, T-ga-0, ml's audit lane for the factor. Order: fourth.

T-ga-4, emitter restructuring. Worktree `claude/emitters`. Files: `src/qd.rs` (`CMA_LIMIT` 1,024; a sample is a 16 B recipe of breed round, slot and state version; `tell` when 200 results of the current version are absorbed, z regenerated from the counter RNG; version bump per tell), `src/storage.rs` (`plan_offspring`: four optimizers per island at once, round robin by slot; tells in absorption order; the save carries emitters and pending recipes). Deliverable: 1,024 emitters with lambda 200. Gate: the samples-per-tell histogram (diagnostic env var) shows 200 for every active emitter; `search_ab --gpu` at equal evaluations over 10 seeds and 40 generations, best at or above 1.1x control and QD at or above 0.95x; below 1.05x the track is deleted. Dependencies: cpu's counter RNG (`claude/breed`) for z regeneration; `qd::VERSION` bumps with it. Order: fifth, in parallel with T-ga-3.

T-ga-5, the growth-step rule. Worktree `claude/growth-rule`. Files: `src/evolution.rs` (`apply_structural_operator`: a child that gains more than 4 nodes or 4 muscles over its parent does not fit, the picker retries as today), `src/evolution/anatomy/mod.rs` (the fit predicate shared by the operators), `docs/anatomy-operators.md`. Deliverable: the tail gone by construction, every operator kept. Gate: ring offspring 17-plus share under 2% and muscle p90 not above the elite p90 plus 4 on a generation-50 ring; `search_ab --gpu` at equal time over 10 seeds, best at or above 0.97x, reserve admissions per generation not below control. Dependencies: T-ga-0's three tail numbers and the owner's yes. Order: after the dump, before W = 4 (ruling 9).

Out of my list, by ruling or by measurement first: the archive-following cap (withdrawn), per-emitter racing bars (now ruling 10's R4 floor, ml's track), surrogate best-of-2 (no Spearman yet), duplicate elimination (measure from the dump; under 1% it stays out).

## 3. Three measurements that would change my mind

1. `dump_stats` entrant recall at R3: if more than 4% of archive entrants sit below 0.7 times their neighbourhood minimum at 5 s, per-cell bars do not predict entry and T-ga-3 is dropped; the design keeps arena bars only and steps per creature stay near 480 at maturity, which moves every row down by 1.6x.
2. `search_ab --gpu` at equal evaluations, 1,024 emitters against 96: best distance under 1.05x of control over 10 seeds. Then the emitter, not the sample count, limits progress, T-ga-4 is deleted, and the "2M evaluations into progress" claim rests on islands and the archive alone.
3. `dump_stats` on the tail: if the 17-plus offspring come mostly from parents already above 13 nodes, or any top-300 elite of the last 20 generations was born more than 4 nodes above its parent, the growth rule does nothing or costs winners; I drop it and pay the tail's 9% of the GPU with W = 8 and R1.

## 4. Honest ceiling

Assumptions: the kernel's harmonic-mean rate over the generation-51 mix with the tail capped, 300M at 3T and 500M at 5T at 2 substeps (chair's table, gpu's numbers); the host tax at 18% (ruling 11); steps per creature 300 with R1 to R3 and 270 with R4 at ruling 10's 50% fire rate, plus the nursery exemption's 5%; L2.5 at 1.6x if it passes; the muscle slope to generation 100 at 0.75x without the growth rule and 0.9x with it.

| case | generation 51, 3T | 51, 5T | generation 100, 3T | 100, 5T |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 2 substeps, R1 to R3 | 0.8M | 1.35M | 0.6 to 0.75M | 1.0 to 1.2M |
| 2 substeps, R1 to R4 | 0.9M | 1.5M | 0.7 to 0.8M | 1.1 to 1.35M |
| L2.5, R1 to R3 | 1.3M | 2.15M | 1.0 to 1.2M | 1.6 to 1.95M |
| L2.5, R1 to R4 | 1.45M | 2.4M | 1.1 to 1.3M | 1.8 to 2.15M |

So 2M/s sustained is reachable at generation 51 only at 5T with L2.5, and at generation 100 only at 5T with L2.5, R4 and the growth rule holding the muscle slope near 0.9x. At 3T this laptop tops out at about 1.45M/s with everything passing. I would tell the owner that plainly: the search side delivers 1.6 to 1.8x of the 25x and the rest is the kernel, the substep and the power cap.

## 5. Ruling 10

I agree. Exempting nurseries and immigrants from R1 and R2 at first costs about 5% of steps and protects the one place where slow starters are the point; ml's per-arena fit can replace the exemption when the dump shows nursery entrants stopped under 0.1% in every band. Flooring R4 at the emitter's own median d(10) is my round-1 racing bar returned as a floor, and it closes the sprinter drift because the top half of every tell is never stopped; the 50% fire rate follows from that floor by construction and I accept it in place of my 5%, with the front-loading bar (mean final / d(10) of entrants within 5% of control) kept as the check.
