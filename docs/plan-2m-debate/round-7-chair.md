The owner removed a premise. AGENTS.md at c198daf: "No golden reference. physics wrote arch/round-7-physics.md on that premise. Read it first. Its proposal, in short:

1. The muscle: node to node (no anchor fractions along rods), force = cap x strength x a(t) x E x max(0, 1 - v / v_max), pull only, a(t) a trapezoid rhythm of period, phase and duty (no cosine, no waveform derivative, no per-muscle state word), one energy store per creature (stamina) scaling every muscle, no tendon (elastic storage moves to a compliance gene on the joint-limit row, a ligament), per-limb touchdown clocks kept, the cap scaled by driven mass and the muscle mass kept. Cost 24 instructions per muscle per substep plus 6 per step: about 32 warp instructions per creature-step (WI) for 19 muscles at 2 substeps against about 100 in the baked estimate of today's model.
2. The contact solve: per-node impulses against the node's own mass for every touching node (no deepest-4), friction clamped per node with the clean rule and the anchor, then the exact chordal rod solve as the coupling, two passes per substep. About 9 WI per substep against 50 for the Delassus matrix plus the active set. Joint limits and the spin cap as per-joint impulses with the ligament compliance folded in. Not Jacobi: each node solves against its own mass and never overshoots; the rod solve does the coupling.
3. Integrator: symplectic Euler, exact rods, the drift projection with infinite-mass feet, the four ledgers, and Macklin's small steps: 4 substeps of 1/240 with a lagged rod factor as the primary target (about 170 WI, 2.4x over 415), 2 substeps of 1/120 as the stretch (125 WI, 3.3x), 1 substep 90 WI. Registers about 90 at W = 2, so W = 1 opens for 70% of offspring (no exchange shuffles, about 15% more).
4. Ceiling at the FMA cap (124 G warp instructions/s), generation-51 mix, 300 steps, 18% host tax: 1.7M/s at 4 substeps, 2.3M/s at 2, 3.2M/s at 1; generation 100 about 0.85x because muscles are a fifth of the step. 2M/s is back in reach: at 4 substeps with R4, at 2 substeps with R1 to R3.
5. Measurements: the stub counts each variant in a day; the lane-group kernel takes the muscle and contact variants behind defines in a week and runs a 30-generation evolution at 3M for the gaits; gates are first_generation, the elite retest at 4x the substep count (median 0.9, p10 reported), slip ratio 0.95 to 1.05, p99 penetration under 2 mm, the four ledgers at 1e-5, and the top-20 replays for the owner's eye with the archive's cost-of-transport spread and the cadence and contact bins filled as the numeric proxy for interesting and efficient. Then the substep ladder on the new physics: evolve at 2 substeps, retest at 8.
