## What round 2 settled (the chair's rulings; design on top of these)

1. The base is 480 steps per creature and 43 to 45M creature-steps/s at the generation-3 body. At the generation-51 body (below) the current kernel would run at about 0.6x of that.
2. The population to design for (ga, measured on save42.evo, generation 51): ring offspring at 8.7 nodes mean, p50 7, p90 12; muscles 19.2 mean, p50 17, p90 29. 70% of offspring have 8 nodes or fewer, 21% have 9 to 16, 9% have 17 to 32 (clusters at 25, 29 and 30). The 9% tail takes about a quarter of all lane-steps and no top-300 elite has more than 9 nodes. Elites: 8.6 nodes mean, p90 11, muscles 19 mean, p90 28; the physics has held elites near 9 nodes since generation 30. Nothing holds offspring.
3. The kernel: per-lane, maximal coordinates with the direct chordal tree solve and a direct contact solve, W = 1 for small bodies and W = 2 as the main class, the lane-group reduced-coordinate kernel as the fallback above the per-lane classes. gpu and physics now agree on this and on the register picture (W = 1 at 6 nodes about 145 registers with the muscle state packed, W = 2 about 95 to 110). Instruction counts per creature-step at 6 nodes and 15 muscles: 3,100 to 3,220 at 1 substep, 5,600 to 6,300 at 2 substeps (both experts). The p90 body (9 nodes, 35 to 40 muscles) costs 5,400 at 1 substep and 10,500 to 12,800 at 2. The formulation itself is worth 1.1x in instructions; its value is the register file and the removal of shuffles and syncwarps.
4. The power budget is unknown within 2.5x: 3T thread instructions/s sustained if the kernel keeps today's MIO-heavy mix, 4.5 to 5.5T if it is FMA-dense (gpu), up to 7.6T at the issue peak. os's calibration (synthetic FMA kernel at the cap, 30 s, clocks and limiter logged) needs the exclusive lock and runs after the debate. Every table in round 3 states its numbers at both 3T and 5T.
5. Steps per creature: one ladder, owned by ga and ml. R1 at 1 s (calibrated stop on running state), R2 at 2.5 s (one checkpoint), R3 at 5 s (per-arena and per-cell bars), R4 at 10 s (elite-relative, neighbourhood minimum, empty cell blocks the stop, optimizer children exempt), plus a 1% audit lane with every rule off. Mean steps: 330 fresh; mature 235 (ga, R4 fires for 5%) to 194 (ml, R4 fires for 85% of survivors at 9 s). The two still differ on R4's fire rate by a factor of 4, and only the dump decides it. For round 3 use 300 mature as the conservative figure (R1 to R3 only) and 235 as the optimistic one. The cheap-fidelity screen (physics) is the fallback if 1 substep fails its honesty test and nets 1.2x on top of R1 to R3, not 1.25x on 480. The 3 s screen, the 2 s rung and the 10 s global rung are withdrawn as separate levers.
6. 1 substep (physics proposal 1: realized-work ledger, anchored friction): 1.9x in joules, 50% odds, decided by a zero-code experiment (EVOLUTION_WARP_SUBSTEPS=1 evolve 30 generations, re-test at 2 and 4) plus 40 and 60 kernel lines. It is a physics change with a posture-adjacent rule (what a planted foot is) and needs the owner.
7. The host: breeding stays on the CPU in cpu's fixed-array form with a counter-based RNG keyed by (seed, generation, breed round, slot, gene, draw); structural operators are conceded to the CPU by os, data and gpu; genes are written once into pinned host memory in SoA form and the RTX unpacks at take-up (Model::new on the device); the descriptor bins and a candidate bit are computed in the kernel against a 29 KB occupant table; the CPU commits candidates in ring order. Recipes for parametric children on the RTX are insurance, built only if breed_bench shows over 8,000 weighted cycles per child or plan_offspring over 2,000. The Radeon pack and Radeon breeding are withdrawn by igpu unless the CPU rewrite fails at more than 3 busy cores. cpu will run EVOLUTION_PROFILE_BREED on save42.evo and report the mature archive and boundary numbers.
8. The ring: depth by measured latency, 5x the p95 per-block host chain, floor 0.3 s, cap 1 s (data withdrew 2 s; ga asked for 0.5 to 1 s). Blocks of 50 ms of GPU work, floor 32k, cap 256k, with per-emitter tells every 200 accumulated samples in ring order (ga withdrew 16 blocks of 49k). The take-up counter spans waves. Today's ring of 786k is 5 to 9x too deep at today's rate and a button press discards 26% of a generation; that is a bug to fix now.
9. The Radeon: physics closed (igpu accepts). igpu built a Radeon compute burner (2.5 TFLOPS standalone, no dispatch over 10 ms, no journal entry) but the rows beside p2_speed produced no usable number: the burner did not run during the row (Radeon busy 0 to 18%) and the "no load" row was contended by an exclusive p2_speed A/B another expert was running (28 to 36M against the 45M reference). So the Radeon power row is still unmeasured and goes on os's table after the debate. The Radeon's remaining roles are the UI (already there) and a documented fallback for breeding. The amdgpu lockup timeout is 2,000 ms on this kernel (os withdrew 10 s).
10. The gaussian and the stream: a changed stream is a new seed (ga). Since one implementation breeds each child, bit equality across devices is not required; if a second breeder is ever built, the rule is integer-exact (cpu and ga: a 12-term sum of 16-bit uniforms from splitmix hashes; igpu prefers a pinned-FMA polynomial and may argue it in round 3 in one paragraph). The chair leans to the integer sum because it needs no contraction rule anywhere.
11. The 13% in p2_speed is host packing and copies, not wave tail. The chair withdraws its "free 1.15x". The tail is about 2% per wave and is hidden by queued waves.
12. Muscle constants at 16 bits (gpu's packed record) is a genome quantization the GA domain must accept; data would not gate the data flow on it. ga: rule on it in round 3.

## Where the multiplication stands (the chair's arithmetic, for everyone to attack)

At the generation-51 mix (60% mean-like, 30% p90-like, 10% large), physics's harmonic-mean kernel rate is about 600M creature-steps/s at 1 substep and 320M at 2 substeps on a 2.8T budget; multiply by about 1.5 at 4.2T. gpu's numbers at the p90 body are 200M (3T) to 340M (5T) at 2 substeps in reduced coordinates. Take the chair's working range for the whole population at 2 substeps: 300 to 450M; at 1 substep: 550 to 800M.

| case | kernel, creature-steps/s | steps per creature | creatures/s |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 2 substeps, R1 to R3 | 300 to 450M | 300 | 1.0 to 1.5M |
| 2 substeps, R1 to R4 at ml's rate | 300 to 450M | 235 | 1.3 to 1.9M |
| 1 substep, R1 to R3 | 550 to 800M | 300 | 1.8 to 2.7M |
| 1 substep, R1 to R4 | 550 to 800M | 235 | 2.3 to 3.4M |

Before any host tax (the CPU at 2 to 3 busy cores at the cap: 5 to 8%) and any body-size drift beyond generation 51. So: 2M/s sustained at generation 50 needs the per-lane kernel plus either 1 substep or the full ladder plus the high power budget, and it needs bodies held near today's generation-51 sizes. With 2 substeps and R1 to R3 only, the honest ceiling is 1.0 to 1.5M/s. Every design must say which row it lands on and what it does if 1 substep fails.
