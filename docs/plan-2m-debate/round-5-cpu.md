# Round 5, CPU domain: convergence

## 1. The design I back

Breeding stays on the CPU: bounded arrays over the 64 operators unchanged, a counter-based RNG keyed by (seed, generation, round, slot, gene, draw) with the 12-uniform gaussian, a 16-lane vector path for parametric children with `repair` still run on every child, operator-sorted and parent-major structural breeding, `plan_offspring` at about 600 cycles per slot, a versioned append-only elite slab per island so breed(k+1) overlaps absorb(k), and a commit on the kernel's candidate bit with the reserve floor as one more word in the occupant table. Genes are written once into process-owned pinned SoA arenas registered with cuMemHostRegister, DMA'd per block into VRAM, unpacked by data's prologue kernel (ruling 2), and taken up from VRAM records. Block size and ring depth are experiment constants saved with the experiment (ruling 4), which my slab design needs anyway, since the plan snapshot a block breeds from is tied to the absorption count. I accept all thirteen rulings. On ruling 11 I note, without rejecting it, that 15 to 21% is the tax at EPP performance with cores boosting to 5 GHz; my derived 7 to 10% assumes os's cap or burst pacing, and the rows decide. On ruling 9 my domain's only stake is that the growth-step rule is a fit check inside the operators, which the bounded-array work already implements as "does not fit, try again".

## 3. Three measurements that would change my mind

1. breed_bench's structural row at the generation-51 mean body (T2). At or below 9,000 cycles the CPU plan stands as written. Above 9,000, os's frequency cap and burst pacing come first (ruling 11). Above 15,000 the host needs more than 4 cores at 2M/s and I back data's structural port on the RTX, weeks and all, because no CPU rewrite short of SoA operators closes a 2.5x gap.
2. os's rows 1 to 3 (GPU rate with 0, 2, 4, 8 busy cores at the cap). If 4 busy cores cost the GPU more than 12%, the recipes insurance opens whatever the parametric row says, because every core-second on the host is then worth more than the port costs. If they cost under 4%, the host tax is noise, T3 and T4 drop behind the kernel tracks in priority, and the tables carry 4%.
3. T0's mature numbers. If the archive stage on save42 exceeds 60 ms per 196k block or the boundary exceeds 300 ms, T6 changes before it starts: incremental novelty and a pool-side boundary become its deliverable rather than an option, and the ring floor rises from 0.3 to 0.5 s. If both are under half those numbers, T6 shrinks to the candidate walk and the reserve word.

## 4. Honest ceiling

The host is not the wall in this design at any row; the ceiling is kernel rate times steps times power times the host tax. Using the chair's kernel ranges (300 to 450M creature-steps/s at 2 substeps for the generation-51 mix), 300 steps for R1 to R3, and the carried host tax of 15 to 21% (factor 0.82), before R4:

| case | generation 51, 3T | generation 51, 5T | generation 100, 3T | generation 100, 5T |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 2 substeps, R1 to R3 | 0.82M | 1.23M | 0.53 to 0.74M | 0.80 to 1.11M |
| L2.5 (1.5 to 1.7x, 65% odds) | 1.23 to 1.39M | 1.85 to 2.09M | 0.80 to 1.25M | 1.20 to 1.88M |
| L2 (1.85x, 45% odds) | 1.52M | 2.28M | 0.99 to 1.37M | 1.48 to 2.05M |

Generation 100 uses os's muscle slope of 0.65 to 0.82x (the low end without the growth-step rule, the high end with it); R4 at the ruled 50% fire rate adds about 1.13x to every cell. Three assumptions set these numbers. First, the kernel reaches 45% issue at 16 warps; at 30% every cell scales by 0.67, and then 2M/s is out of reach at generation 51 on any row but L2 at 5T. Second, the host tax is 15 to 21% as carried; if os's rows put it at my derived 7 to 10%, every cell gains about 1.1x. Third, the owner accepts the growth-step rule, or the generation-100 column is its low end. So: 2M/s sustained at generation 51 needs the 5T budget and L2.5 or L2; at generation 100 it needs 5T, L2 or L2.5's top end, R4 firing, and the growth rule. At 3T the honest sustained number at generation 51 is 1.2 to 1.5M/s with a passing substep rung and 0.8M/s without one. Nothing my domain can build moves these cells by more than the tax factor, and that is the number I most want measured.
