# Round 5, chair: convergence

Theme: each expert states the design they now back, in their own words, and the measurements that would change their mind. Write arch/round-5-<domain>.md, under 1,500 words. This is the last scheduled round; if a lever worth more than 1.5x is still contested after it, the chair runs one more round on that lever alone.

## The chair's rulings from round 4

The red team found eleven real defects and closed most of them in the same round. These are now part of the design and the plan will state them as such.

1. In-kernel confirmation (gpu's round-3 idea) is out. data and ml showed it makes the fine trial a second implementation (the cascade path still runs the compiled fine kernel), so a record's min(standard, confirm) would depend on which path ran, which depends on timing; it adds six live registers to the tightest kernel; the replay of a fine elite would not match its score unless a third variant existed; and the nursery graduation makes records fall, so the stale-table superset argument fails there. data's 3 reserved slots for a persistent fine-fidelity kernel plus 1 replay slot are in (3 to 4% of the GPU). Revisit only if the measured per-block chain p95 with those slots puts the ring above 0.5 s.
2. Zero-copy gene reads at take-up are out. gpu showed a take-up is a divergent branch that stalls the whole warp (4 to 13% of throughput), and data showed an in-kernel unpack with one lane active costs about 12% of issue slots in a per-lane kernel. The design is: DMA the block's genes into VRAM on a copy engine at submit, a per-block prologue kernel that unpacks 32 creatures per warp at full lane use into lane records in VRAM, and take-up from VRAM records. VRAM budget about 2 GB at a 1 s ring. The one-day measurement (p2_speed on save42 with records from VRAM, records zero-copy, genes plus in-kernel unpack) still runs to confirm.
3. The mapped-word spin is out. 4,608 pollers at 2 us each would flood the PCIe link and slow the DMA that ends the starvation. The queue tail and the drain flag live in VRAM and the host writes them with cuStreamWriteValue32 on the submit stream. A 2 s drain timeout destroys the context from another thread if a kernel never observes the flag.
4. The ring depth and the block size are not runtime measurements. ml's stop-ship stands: a child is bred at its block's absorption from the archive as it stands then, and how many blocks were absorbed before it depends on how many were in flight and how large, so two runs of one seed at different measured rates would produce different histories. Block size and ring depth are experiment constants chosen at experiment start (from the first measured rate), saved in the experiment, and changed only at a generation boundary with the value recorded in the history. The engine still measures the rate for the stage log; it does not act on it inside a generation.
5. Bucketed queues need three rules, now in the design: a retiring lane checks the tail before its atomicAdd and, if it has already claimed a ref when it sees the drain flag, runs it anyway and tags the result with the old world; the host requeues only after the kernel has exited (event, not flag), resets head to tail, and re-appends every ref whose result sentinel is unset; every non-empty bucket gets at least one warp at launch and a lane whose bucket is dry takes from the next bucket up within its class.
6. Persistent kernels and faults. Arenas and the results ring are process memory registered with cuMemHostRegister, never driver-allocated, so a context loss frees nothing on the host; recovery is reopen, cached cubins, re-register, head = tail, re-append the blocks whose completion words are unset, relaunch; blocks re-run whole from the same genes, so the history is invariant. After three failures the game pauses with a message, because the CPU engine is being deleted and there is no failover physics; docs/building.md says so. Whether the CUDA engine lives in a helper process (igpu: the only way to kill a hung persistent kernel on a GPU with no display watchdog) or in-process with the 2 s drain timeout (data, os) is the one open engineering choice in this item; the data expert decides it in round 5 with one number: whether cuCtxDestroy can be made to return on a hung kernel in-process.
7. Physics spec changes from the red team, now in the spec: contact nodes with a valid anchor or an active contact have infinite mass in the drift projection (physics, closing the planted-foot slip ga and physics both found); penetration recovery becomes a split impulse (position only, never momentum); the tendon becomes a compliant row (it was explicit at omega DT of 4.7 at 1/60 s); the realized-work ledger extends to the projection's displacement times rod tension (gpu); the flight-step first-law allowance tightens to 5e-5 + 5e-6 x scale. Open: ga's angular-momentum argument. A position-only projection removes a share of a swinging limb's angular momentum with no change to energy or linear momentum. physics's infinite-mass-foot rule stops the foot moving; it does not restore the limb's angular momentum. physics must say in round 5 whether a fourth ledger (angular momentum about the center of mass across internal operations, bar 1e-5 per step) is in the spec and the audit, or why the infinite-mass rule makes it unnecessary.
8. The substep ladder gains a rung. physics's L2.5, spin-adaptive substeps (2 substeps when any rod spins above 5 rad/s or any contact candidate approaches above 0.5 m/s, else 1), state-only, one physics, fixed thresholds like the spin cap, at 65% odds and 1.5 to 1.7x against L2's 45% and 1.85x. The ladder is L0, L1, L2, L2.5, each with the 0.9 median honesty bar and p10 reported. The plan's primary target row is L2.5.
9. The body cap. ga withdrew the archive-following cap (it holds a reserve plan below what it needs to grow) for a growth-step rule: a child gains at most 4 nodes and 4 muscles over its parent, a limit in the operators' fit check like the bone length cap, every operator kept. physics wants no cap until the dump shows whether any top-300 elite of the last 20 generations was born above its island's incumbent plus two. igpu's alternative is a hard 16-node offspring cap that deletes W = 8 and the lane-group kernel. os wants whatever rule exists to count muscles (the generation-100 slope is 0.65 to 0.82x of generation 51 by muscles alone). Ruling: the growth-step rule in nodes and muscles is the candidate, it is the owner's decision, and the dump runs first with three tail numbers (R1 stop share, entrant share by archive including the reserve, operator histogram). W = 8 is built last, after W = 2 and W = 4; until it exists the lane-group kernel serves the tail, which is why the growth rule matters early.
10. The ladder and the nursery. Nursery and immigrant slots are exempt from R2 (every file agrees). For R1: ga and physics exempt, ml fits per arena type with a 1-in-10,000 budget and an absolute breaker. Ruling: exempt at first (cost about 5% of steps); ml's per-arena fit comes in only when the dump shows nursery entrants stopped under 0.1% in every band. R4 fires only for a sample below its emitter's own median d(10) (physics's floor, which also answers the sprinter drift), and ml's stale() bound (5 stagnant tells recycles the emitter) is the second guard. R4's expected fire rate is then about 50% of survivors, between ga's 5% and ml's 85%.
11. The host tax is carried at 15 to 21% in every table until os's rows 1 to 3 exist (cores at EPP performance boost to 5 GHz). The decision at cpu's breed_bench gate follows cpu's and gpu's amended rule: a structural row above 9,000 cycles opens the frequency cap and burst pacing first; a parametric row above 3,000 opens recipes on the RTX. The orchestrator decides with the rows in hand, about two weeks in.
12. Smaller items now in the design: kernel compiles of the three standard classes in parallel on a world change, neighbour-world prefetch (the 14 single-toggle worlds) in the background, a visible "compiling the new world" state, age eviction of the kernel cache at 200 files, and the fat-kernel measurement (effects as uniform runtime parameters; adopt if under 3% on p2_speed). feet() counts lift_lo only; the prototype writes the same seven Result words the kernel writes; the audit window is saved as 1,024-bin histograms (128 KB). Contact rows unrolled at a fixed MAXC with predication (runtime-indexed rows go to local memory whatever the register count). The class is max(ceil(nodes / 4), ceil(muscles / 16)). Arenas grow one block ahead of their use. No Instant::now in per-creature loops. The stage log prints mean and p90 nodes and muscles per generation and starved seconds per block. The reserve floor is one more word in the occupant table so the candidate bit does not starve the morphology reserve. physics_audit runs on every new island record once per generation, and the audit lane's entrants are re-run at 4x before commit (a few hundred per generation), which is the only guard that watches the physics rather than the search.
13. Disk. os measured 74 worktrees and 244 GB of target directories at 98% full early in round 4; cpu measured 51% full with 220 GB free at the end of it (about 210 GB was freed during the debate; 4 of 70 worktrees still carry targets, 23 GB; the filesystem is ext4, so a reflink seed is a full 4.5 GB copy). The plan's rules: prune the finished worktrees, remove a worktree the day its branch merges, at most 10 worktrees at once, and an agent refuses to seed a worktree below 30 GB free. The kernel cache (498 MB, no eviction) gets the cap in item 12.

## What is still contested and by how much

- The power budget, 3T against 5T: a 1.6x factor on every row. Not arguable; os's rows 11 and 12 decide it in one 5-minute pause after the debate.
- The substep: L2.5 at 65% odds for 1.5 to 1.7x, L2 at 45% for 1.85x, neither for 1x. Not arguable; the ladder runs on the current kernel in a day.
- The kernel's issue rate at 16 warps: 30% against 45%, a 1.5x factor. Not arguable; the stub kernel and a 30-minute nsys warp-state sample on the lane-group kernel (share of stalls that are barrier and short-scoreboard) decide it.
- R4's fire rate: 5% (ga) against 85% (ml), now about 50% under the emitter-median floor; a 1.28x factor at most. Below the 1.5x threshold; the dump decides it.
- The muscle slope past generation 51: 0.65x to 0.82x by generation 100. Below the threshold; the stage log line and the growth rule decide it.

So no lever above 1.5x is contested on argument; every one is contested on a measurement that runs after the debate. Unless round 5 reopens one, the debate closes after this round.
